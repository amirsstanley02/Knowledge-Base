# Overwhelm & Time Management System

A working system for when work and tasks have piled up past what feels manageable.
The goal isn't to do everything — it's to make the pile visible, cut it down, and
protect a fixed block of free time every week.

## The core idea

Overwhelm usually isn't a "not enough hours" problem. It's a "too many open loops
with no single place to see them" problem. This system fixes that in four steps:

1. **Capture** everything in one place so nothing is being held in your head.
2. **Triage** it against one matrix so priority stops being a daily argument with yourself.
3. **Plan** a small number of things per day/week, not the whole list.
4. **Protect** free time as a scheduled, non-negotiable block — not "whatever's left over."

## How to use this folder

| File | Purpose | When to use it |
|---|---|---|
| [`GUIDE.md`](./GUIDE.md) | The actual tactics: triage rules, saying no, delegating, batching | Read once, re-read when stuck |
| [`templates/inbox.md`](./templates/inbox.md) | Dump every task/commitment here as it comes in | Every time something new lands on you |
| [`templates/priority-matrix.md`](./templates/priority-matrix.md) | Sort the inbox into Do / Schedule / Delegate / Drop | Once a day or every other day |
| [`templates/weekly-plan.md`](./templates/weekly-plan.md) | Pick the week's 3-5 real priorities + protect free time | Sunday night or Monday morning |
| [`templates/daily-plan.md`](./templates/daily-plan.md) | Pick today's max 3 things | Start of each day |
| [`templates/time-audit.md`](./templates/time-audit.md) | Find out where your hours are actually going | Once, when things feel out of control |

## Quick start (do this now)

1. Copy `templates/inbox.md` to a new file (e.g. `2026-09-inbox.md`) and dump
   **everything** currently on your mind — every task, ask, commitment, "I should really..."
   Don't sort yet, just get it out of your head.
2. Run it through `templates/priority-matrix.md`. Be honest about the "Drop" and
   "Delegate" columns — most overwhelm lists have more droppable items than people admit.
3. Fill out `templates/weekly-plan.md`, and put your free-time block on it **first**,
   before work tasks. Treat it like a meeting you can't move.
4. Each morning, fill out `templates/daily-plan.md` with a max of 3 things.

Re-run the audit whenever the list starts feeling unmanageable again — that's the
signal to re-triage, not to grind harder.
