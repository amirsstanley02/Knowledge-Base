# Time Audit — Where Is the Time Actually Going?

Do this once when things feel unmanageable, before making any big changes.
For 3-5 days, log time in rough 30-60 min blocks. Don't optimize yet, just observe.

## After 3-5 days, answer honestly

- Where did most of the hours actually go? Does it match what you assumed?
- What took much longer than it should have?
- What showed up repeatedly that could be batched, automated, templated, or delegated?
- How many hours of real free time were there, actually — not "leftover time
  that got absorbed by something else"?
- What's the single biggest time sink that, if cut in half, would free up the
  most hours?

## One change to make this week

Pick exactly one finding above and turn it into an action in
`weekly-plan.md` (e.g. "batch email to 2x/day", "delegate X recurring task",
"decline meetings without an agenda"). One real change beats five vague
intentions.
