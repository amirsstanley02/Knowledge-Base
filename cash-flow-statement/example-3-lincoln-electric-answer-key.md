## Step 2: Depreciation check (does NOT tie)
- Income statement D&A: 98,546, which includes 29,717 of intangible amortization.

- Change in accumulated depreciation: 77,172
- Difference: 8,343. This comes from currency translation of foreign plants, plus disposals. Birch's "no PP&E sold or retired" assumption is what made its numbers tie, and this company does not meet it.

## Real-world note
The company's own reported cash flow statement shows operating 661,173, investing (257,326), financing (461,887) and an FX effect on cash of (10,433) (net change -68,473). Your derived sections will differ from those. That is expected: a statement built only from balance sheet changes can't separate acquisitions, currency effects and non-cash items the way the company's own records can. The total change in cash is the part that must always tie.
